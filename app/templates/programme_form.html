Item shape

product
{% for p in products %}{{ p }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}
kind
{% for k in kinds %}{{ k }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}
value
The product's own name for the right: pro_buyer (PP Hunter), starter (Ordris plan), area (HNH seat), mentee (KP sourcer source), kpuk.submit (PP flag), affiliate (PP affiliate programme), partner (Ordris partner programme).
months
Optional whole number from 1 to 13. With it the item runs that many months from the start date; without it the item follows the mentee's computed access end.
extras
fee_share_pct (0 to 100) on a kp sourcer_link; area_slug or postcodes (a list of outward codes) and fee_waived (true or false) on an hnh partner_seat. A template may give one pp comp_tier only.
affiliate
pp affiliate and ordris affiliate enrol the person in that product's public affiliate or partner programme, once each. Membership is permanent, so a revoke is recorded here and nothing is sent.
